# SME AI Safety Toolkit

Free, practical tools to help small and medium businesses adopt AI safely and securely: checklists, AI skill files and step-by-step procedures on data privacy, cyber security, IP, staff and customer risk, and governance.

**Live site:** https://himjoe.github.io/safe-ai-sme-toolkit/

Prepared by **SafeAlign AI** · questions and suggestions: info@safealignai.io

## What is in here

| Tool | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Safe AI Adoption Checklist (interactive) | 26 items across 7 sections, with notes and a sign-off record. Answers stay in your browser. | `checklist/` |
| Safe AI Adoption Checklist (PDF) | Printable two-page version | `skills/safe-ai-adoption-checklist/` |
| Safe AI Adoption Checklist (Word) | Editable version for your own use | `skills/safe-ai-adoption-checklist/` |
| AI skill file (`SKILL.md`) | Load into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI assistant to run the checklist as a guided walkthrough | `skills/safe-ai-adoption-checklist/` |
| Standard Operating Procedure | How to set up the skill, run a review, decide go-live and keep records | `skills/safe-ai-adoption-checklist/SOP.docx` |

Coming next: cyber basics for SMEs, AI vendor review, AI incident response.

## How to use

1. Pick one low-risk AI use case.
2. Open the [interactive checklist](https://himjoe.github.io/safe-ai-sme-toolkit/checklist/), or load `SKILL.md` into your AI assistant.
3. Fix the red flags first. They block go-live.
4. Record the owner and a 90-day review date.

## Disclaimer

This is practical guidance, not legal advice. PIPEDA, Ontario human-rights, AODA and contract obligations should be confirmed with a qualified professional.
