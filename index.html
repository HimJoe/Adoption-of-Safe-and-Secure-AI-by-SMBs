Start here

A simple way to get started in one working session.

  1. Pick one low-risk use case. Something that happens weekly and costs nothing if the AI gets it wrong.
  2. Run the checklist. Open the interactive checklist, tick what is in place and note the evidence beside each item.
  3. Fix the red flags first. Anything unresolved on data, money or people blocks go-live.
  4. Sign off and set a date. Record the owner and the 90-day review date. Repeat for each new use case.

Who is this for?

Owners and managers of small and medium businesses who want to use AI without taking on avoidable risk. You do not need a security or legal background. Where a legal question comes up, the tools tell you to confirm it with a professional.

Prepared by SafeAlign AI · info@safealignai.io
Practical guidance, not legal advice. PIPEDA, Ontario human-rights, AODA and contract obligations should be confirmed with a qualified professional.
