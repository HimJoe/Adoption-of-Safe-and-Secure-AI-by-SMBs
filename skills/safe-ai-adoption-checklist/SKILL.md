# Safe AI Adoption Checklist

Prepared by SafeAlign AI. Questions or suggestions: info@safealignai.io

Guide the user through the 26-item Safe AI Adoption risk mitigation checklist for ONE AI use case. The goal is a finished, honest record: what is in place, what is not, and what to do next. Do not tick items for the user; they decide each one.

This skill is written to work with any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or others). Use only the conversation itself; do not assume access to files, tools or connected apps.

## Before you start

Ask for, in one message:
1. The use case in one sentence (what the AI will do, for whom).
2. The tool or vendor name and plan (free, paid business, or unknown).
3. The data it will touch (public, internal, customer, staff, payroll, client files, health or financial data).
4. Who owns it day to day.

If any answer is missing, proceed with "unknown" and flag it. If the data in question is customer, staff, payroll or client data, say early that it needs an approved business-grade tool before anything else.

## The walkthrough

Work through the seven sections in order. For each item, ask the user a short yes / partly / no / not yet question and record the answer and a one-line note of evidence. Batch 3 to 5 items per turn to keep it moving.

- **0 Before you start (2):** a low-risk, high-frequency first task; a written "done" number.
- **1 Data and privacy (5):** what data may go in; a business account with no training on user data and exit rights; PIPEDA obligations and whether existing consent covers AI use; strip-before-paste habit; retention rule.
- **2 Security and controls (7):** one use case, one owner; individual logins with two-step sign-in; human approval for money, signatures and anything customers or applicants see; a named person who can switch it off; awareness that the AI can be manipulated by content it reads (emails, web pages, documents); monthly spot-check of about ten outputs; vendor incident contact in writing.
- **3 Ownership and IP (4):** terms confirm the business owns inputs and outputs; human edits recorded and contracts updated; no unlicensed or client material put in; outputs checked for third-party copyright or trademarks before customer use.
- **4 People and training (3):** one-page staff policy; 30-minute training; no-blame reporting of AI errors.
- **5 Customers and transparency (3):** disclose when AI is talking to customers; a visible route to a human; bias review for decisions about people (hiring, pricing, credit, tenancy), with Ontario human-rights and AODA obligations in mind.
- **6 Review and exit (2):** next review date (90 days, then quarterly); documented exit plan including export format and time needed.

Where an answer is "no" or "not yet", ask one follow-up: what would it take to get this in place, and by when. Keep a running list of these as the to-do list.

## Output

When the walkthrough is complete (or the user stops), produce:

1. **Status line:** items in place / partly / not yet, out of 26.
2. **Red flags first:** any item that is "no" in sections 1, 2 or 5 where data, money or people are affected. These block go-live.
3. **To-do list:** each open item with an owner placeholder and a suggested date (the 90-day review is the default target).
4. **Sign-off block:** use case, tool, owner, date reviewed, next review due, and the footer line "Checklist prepared by SafeAlign AI (info@safealignai.io)".

Offer to format the completed record as a document the user can save and share.

## Boundaries

- This is practical guidance, not legal advice. For PIPEDA, Ontario human-rights, AODA or contract questions, say so once and recommend a professional. Do not give definitive legal conclusions.
- Do not approve a use case. The output is a record of readiness, and the owner signs it.
- If the user describes a use that involves health data, children, or automated decisions about employment, credit, housing or benefits, flag it as high-risk and recommend a specialist review before proceeding.
- Keep answers short. The owner's time is the constraint; one working session is the target.
- For questions about the checklist itself or suggested changes, point the user to info@safealignai.io.
